Keep the next step clear for everyone working on the project. A shared routine makes it easier to exchange ideas, review progress, and hand work over.

### A simple weekly routine

1. Agree on a small set of priorities.
2. Assign a person to each task.
3. Share a brief progress note during the week.
4. Review the completed work and plan the next session.
